The BizFlow workflow

The product follows the work, not the menu.

Every core action contributes to one connected workforce record. That gives HR a clear operating path from hiring through payroll and gives employees a simple place to manage their own work information.

01

Build people

Create employees, assignments, roles, documents, and schedules.

02

Capture time

Record attendance, leave, shifts, overtime, and exceptions.

03

Calculate pay

Bring workforce activity, compensation, loans, and adjustments into payroll.

04

Control payroll

Review confidence, resolve exceptions, approve, and lock the period.

05

Prove the result

Deliver payslips, reports, remittance outputs, and an audit trail.

Security + control

Login once. Resolve the organization. Enforce it everywhere.

BizFlow treats organization membership as the boundary for workforce data and actions. The same identity drives HRIS access and employee timekeeping.

01

Tenant-scoped identity

A user's active organization membership determines the workspace they can access. Multi-organization accounts explicitly select a permitted organization rather than guessing.

02

Time clock follows the employee

The time clock authenticates the same account, resolves the employee's organization, and records attendance against that employee's organization.

03

Controlled payroll

Payroll states, permissions, confidence checks, approvals, and audit events keep sensitive processing inside the organization boundary.

BizFlow

Give your workforce one place to work.

Clients do not need a separate client website to reach HRIS. They use the BizFlow website, sign in with their credentials, and are routed into the organization workspace their account is authorized to use.
