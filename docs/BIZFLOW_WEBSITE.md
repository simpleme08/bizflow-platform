# BizFlow Website Positioning

## Primary slogan

**Run your people. Run your business.**

This replaces the previous "People operations. Connected." headline and positions BizFlow as an operating platform rather than only an HR tool.

## Website direction

- No public plan pricing is displayed.
- Visitors are directed to contact BizFlow for business inquiries, demos, onboarding and partnerships.
- The homepage uses a polished depth/3D visual language: perspective dashboard, floating information card, layered surfaces, soft shadows and responsive motion.
- The product story follows a simple flow: People → Time → Payroll.
- The BizFlow website is the single public entry point for HRIS sign-in and the employee time clock. Customer-specific websites are outside this repository.

## Inquiry CTA

The homepage uses an email inquiry CTA and intentionally avoids presenting plan prices. Replace the inquiry email address with the production BizFlow sales/contact mailbox when that mailbox is finalized.
