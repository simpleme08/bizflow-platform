# BizFlow design system

## Global product direction

BizFlow is positioned and presented as a **futuristic, premium enterprise SaaS platform**.

This direction applies across the project: marketing pages, portals, HRIS workspace screens, dashboards, onboarding, payroll, workforce operations, documentation, and future modules.

### Experience principles

- **Futuristic:** modern interfaces, clear hierarchy, purposeful depth, subtle motion, and technology-forward presentation.
- **Premium:** restrained layouts, strong typography, polished surfaces, intentional spacing, and high-quality interaction states.
- **Enterprise:** communicate control, reliability, security, auditability, compliance, scalability, and operational intelligence.
- **Product-first:** describe capabilities in terms of business outcomes and connected workflows rather than generic HR-software language.
- **Clarity over decoration:** visual effects should support comprehension and hierarchy, not become the product.

## Brand boundaries

Client workspaces retain their own brand identity.

**Do not replace or normalize a client's color palette, logo, or brand-specific visual language.** The futuristic/premium/enterprise positioning may be expressed through layout, typography, component quality, motion, imagery, and copy while the client's established colors remain intact.

This applies to existing clients such as HSIS and to future client websites and branded experiences.

## Content direction

Prefer language such as:

- people operations
- workforce intelligence
- operational control
- connected workflows
- payroll confidence
- enterprise-ready
- compliance-aware
- real-time visibility
- audit-ready
- scalable operations

Avoid positioning BizFlow as a basic employee database, timesheet, or traditional HR portal.

## Visual direction

The core BizFlow product may use:

- deep neutral/navy foundations
- restrained cyan/blue or similarly modern accents
- glass and layered surfaces used selectively
- subtle gradients and depth
- generous whitespace
- crisp borders and soft shadows
- responsive layouts
- reduced-motion support

Client-specific color systems are exceptions and must remain intact.

## Marketing visualization rule

Marketing mockups must not imply that a decorative metric is live production data. Avoid unexplained floating widgets or fake real-time indicators such as a standalone "Live Workforce" card. If a visualization uses sample data, label it as a product concept, preview, or illustrative dashboard where needed.
